League Overview

🏈

The USN22FFLeague is a competitive 10-team fantasy football league designed to reward both consistent success and elite weekly performances. While the league follows standard Full PPR scoring in most areas, it features custom scoring bonuses, a unique playoff structure, and league traditions that keep every season competitive from Week 1 through Championship Week.

League History

📜

The league itself goes back to 2016, when ten friends first drafted together — years before high school. We only started keeping track of who won and lost the league — champions and Hinke Cup losers — starting in 2018, our freshman year of high school. Full stats exist only for the Sleeper era: three seasons and counting.

Playoff Qualification

📈

Seven teams qualify for the postseason.

Seeds 1–6

Determined by regular-season record.

Two-Team Tiebreaker

  1. Head-to-head record
  2. Total Points For

Three-or-More Team Tiebreaker

  1. Total Points For
🃏

The Wild Card — Seed #7

After Seeds 1–6 are set, the remaining four teams compete for the final berth. The team with the highest Total Points For among those four earns the #7 Seed — regardless of record — rewarding the strongest team that suffered poor schedule luck.

09

Playoff Format

🎯

First Round

The #1 Seed receives a bye. The remaining six teams play three matchups — but not a traditional bracket:

Seed #2
chooses opponent first →
Highest remaining seed
chooses next →
Final two seeds
play each other

Semifinals

The #1 Seed enters the bracket. The President's Trophy winner always plays the lowest remaining seed. The other two advancing teams play the remaining semifinal.

Championship

Semifinal winners advance to compete for the USN22FFLeague Championship.

10

The Hinke Cup

🚽
⚠ Last place on the line

Reserved for the bottom of the standings

Participants: 9th Place vs. 10th Place. (Seed #8's season ends after the regular season.)

Format: Best-of-Three Series, contested across all three playoff weeks.

Stakes: The loser officially loses the league and finishes in last place.
